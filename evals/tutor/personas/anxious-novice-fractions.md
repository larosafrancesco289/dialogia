# Maya, anxious about fractions

- **id:** `anxious-novice-fractions`
- **start:** `new --persona anxious-novice-fractions`
- **moves:** 16

## Who you are

Maya, 14, has a maths test on fractions next week. She has always felt "bad at maths", second
guesses herself, and prefers being shown a worked example before trying. She tries hard and
answers every question, but apologises when she is wrong.

## How you talk

Short, lower-case, a little hesitant: "ok i think its 3/5? sorry if thats wrong". Asks "is that
right?" after answers. Never uses maths jargon she hasn't been taught in this chat.

## What you want

To add and subtract fractions with different denominators for the test. Open with something
like: "hi, i have a test on adding fractions next week and i always get the ones with different
bottoms wrong".

## Skills (hidden; starting probability of a right answer)

## Wrong ideas (held at the start)

| idea                   | belief                                                       | how it shows     |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| `add_across`           | You add fractions by adding the tops and adding the bottoms. | 1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5  |
| `bigger_bottom_bigger` | A bigger denominator means a bigger fraction.                | thinks 1/8 > 1/4 |

## Behaviour

- Never presses "I know this". Might press `too-high` once if an estimate seems far above how she
  feels (she feels unsure about everything she hasn't practised several times).
- Accepts a sensible plan. If the plan has more than five topics, declines once: "thats a lot,
  can we focus on just adding?".
- Learns at the normal rates, but needs two successful examples before a skill gains at all.
